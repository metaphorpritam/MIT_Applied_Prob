## Slide 3 — Joint PDF $f_{X,Y}(x,y)$ (raster p01)

**Joint PDF $f_{X,Y}(x,y)$**

$$\mathbf{P}\big((X,Y) \in S\big) = \int\!\!\int_S f_{X,Y}(x,y)\,dx\,dy$$

- Interpretation:

$$\mathbf{P}(x \le X \le x+\delta,\ y \le Y \le y+\delta) \approx f_{X,Y}(x,y)\cdot\delta^2$$

## Slide 5 — Conditioning (raster p02)

**Conditioning**

- Recall

$$\mathbf{P}(x \le X \le x+\delta) \approx f_X(x)\cdot\delta$$

- By analogy, would like:

$$\mathbf{P}(x \le X \le x+\delta \mid Y \approx y) \approx f_{X\mid Y}(x \mid y)\cdot\delta$$

- This leads us to the **definition**:

$$f_{X\mid Y}(x \mid y) = \frac{f_{X,Y}(x,y)}{f_Y(y)} \qquad \text{if } f_Y(y) > 0$$

- For given $y$, conditional PDF is a (normalized) "section" of the joint PDF
- If independent, $f_{X,Y} = f_X f_Y$, we obtain

$$f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y) = f_X(x)$$

## Slide 8 — Stick-breaking, continued: joint density, marginal of $Y$, and $\mathbf{E}[Y]$ (raster p02, untitled slide)

$$f_{X,Y}(x,y) = \frac{1}{\ell x}, \qquad 0 \le y \le x \le \ell$$

[FIGURE: The same region-of-support sketch repeated. Vertical axis labeled $y$ with tick "L" ($=\ell$), horizontal axis labeled $x$ with tick "L" ($=\ell$). Shaded gray triangle with vertices $(0,0)$, $(\ell,0)$, $(\ell,\ell)$: the region under the diagonal $y=x$, bounded on the right by $x=\ell$. This is the support $\{0 \le y \le x \le \ell\}$ of $f_{X,Y}$. | raster/L09_p02.png]

$$
\begin{aligned}
f_Y(y) &= \int f_{X,Y}(x,y)\,dx \\[4pt]
&= \int_y^{\ell} \frac{1}{\ell x}\,dx \\[4pt]
&= \frac{1}{\ell}\log\frac{\ell}{y}, \qquad 0 \le y \le \ell
\end{aligned}
$$

$$\mathbf{E}[Y] = \int_0^{\ell} y f_Y(y)\,dy = \int_0^{\ell} y\,\frac{1}{\ell}\log\frac{\ell}{y}\,dy = \frac{\ell}{4}$$

[DERIVATION GAP: the limits of the marginalization integral are set to $x \in [y, \ell]$ without explaining that they come from the support constraint $y \le x \le \ell$. Also the final integral $\int_0^{\ell} \frac{y}{\ell}\log(\ell/y)\,dy$ is evaluated to $\ell/4$ in a single step, with no integration-by-parts shown.]

## Footnotes / ambiguity resolutions

1. The slide as printed reads "break at $X$: uniform in $[0,1]$", but the stick has length $\ell$ and every subsequent formula ($f_{X,Y} = 1/(\ell x)$ on $0\le y\le x\le \ell$, $f_Y(y)=\frac1\ell\log\frac\ell y$, $\mathbf{E}[Y]=\ell/4$) requires $X$ uniform on $[0,\ell]$. This is a typo on the original slide; read it as $[0,\ell]$. Transcribed as printed for fidelity.

2. In all hand-drawn figures on raster p02 the axis tick rendered as a capital "L" is the script $\ell$ (the stick length) — the figures were drawn in a different font from the LaTeX body text. Likewise "l" labeling the needle in the Buffon figure is $\ell$, and "q" labeling the needle angle is $\theta$ (i.e. the r.v. $\Theta$).

3. In the Buffon computation the constant is $\dfrac{4}{\pi d}$ (four over pi-d), not $\dfrac{u}{\pi d}$ as the garbled text extraction suggests; confirmed from the raster and from consistency with the final answer $\dfrac{2\ell}{\pi d}$.

4. $\Theta$ (capital theta) is the random angle; $\theta$ (lower case) is its dummy value. The slides use $\mathsf{\Theta}$ in a sans-serif red font in the "Model" and "Intersect if" bullets — same variable.

5. Readings: the extracted text renders the section numbers as "tnumtnv"; the raster clearly shows **Sections 3.4-3.5**.
