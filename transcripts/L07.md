## Slide 4 — Expectations (raster p01)

$$\mathbf{E}[X] = \sum_x x\, p_X(x)$$

$$\mathbf{E}[g(X,Y)] = \sum_x \sum_y g(x,y)\, p_{X,Y}(x,y)$$

- In general: $\mathbf{E}[g(X,Y)] \neq g\big(\mathbf{E}[X], \mathbf{E}[Y]\big)$
- $\mathbf{E}[\alpha X + \beta] = \alpha \mathbf{E}[X] + \beta$
- $\mathbf{E}[X + Y + Z] = \mathbf{E}[X] + \mathbf{E}[Y] + \mathbf{E}[Z]$
- If $X$, $Y$ are independent:
  - $\mathbf{E}[XY] = \mathbf{E}[X]\mathbf{E}[Y]$
  - $\mathbf{E}[g(X)h(Y)] = \mathbf{E}[g(X)] \cdot \mathbf{E}[h(Y)]$

[DERIVATION GAP: linearity of expectation and the product rules under independence are asserted; the slide shows no proof (no expansion $\sum_x\sum_y xy\,p_X(x)p_Y(y)$).]

### Footnotes on ambiguous symbols

1. Slide 4: the scalars in $\mathbf{E}[\alpha X + \beta] = \alpha\mathbf{E}[X] + \beta$ are Greek $\alpha$ and $\beta$ (not Latin $a$, $b$); slide 5 by contrast uses Latin $a$ in $\mathrm{Var}(aX)=a^2\mathrm{Var}(X)$ and $\mathrm{Var}(X+a)$. Both readings are unambiguous in the raster.
2. Slide 6: the expectation on the binomial sum line is typeset as plain $E[X]$ (upright italic $E$), whereas the bulleted lines below use boldface $\mathbf{E}[\cdot]$. This is a typesetting inconsistency in the original, not a different operator.
3. Slide 8: the summation index reads $i,j : i \neq j$ (colon, not semicolon) — ordered pairs, hence $n(n-1)$ terms.
4. Slide 3: cells left blank in the joint-PMF grid are genuinely empty in the raster (zero probability), not faint fractions.
