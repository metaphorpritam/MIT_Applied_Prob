## Slide 3 — Finite state Markov chains (raster p01)

- $X_n$: state after $n$ transitions
  - belongs to a finite set, e.g., $\{1, \ldots, m\}$
  - $X_0$ is either given or random
- **Markov property/assumption:**
  (given current state, the past does not matter)

$$
\begin{aligned}
p_{ij} &= \mathbf{P}(X_{n+1} = j \mid X_n = i) \\
       &= \mathbf{P}(X_{n+1} = j \mid X_n = i,\; X_{n-1}, \ldots, X_0)
\end{aligned}
$$

- Model specification:
  - identify the possible states
  - identify the possible transitions
  - identify the transition probabilities

## Slide 4 — $n$-step transition probabilities (raster p01)

- State occupancy probabilities, given initial state $i$:

$$
r_{ij}(n) = \mathbf{P}(X_n = j \mid X_0 = i)
$$

[FIGURE: A three-column trellis / fan diagram illustrating the recursion. Column headers
printed above, left to right: "Time 0", "Time n-1", "Time n". At the far left a single circle
is the state $i$ at time 0. In the middle column there is a vertical stack of circles
representing all possible states $k = 1, \ldots, m$ at time $n-1$: a top circle (state 1), a
vertical ellipsis, a middle circle (state $k$), another vertical ellipsis, and a bottom circle
(state $m$). At the far right a single circle is state $j$ at time $n$. Three straight arrows
fan out from the left circle to the three drawn middle circles, labelled (top to bottom)
$r_{i1}(n-1)$, $r_{ik}(n-1)$, $r_{im}(n-1)$. Three straight arrows converge from those middle
circles onto the right circle, labelled (top to bottom) $p_{1j}$, $p_{kj}$, $p_{mj}$. The
picture depicts summing over the state occupied at time $n-1$. | raster/L16_p01.png]

  - Key recursion:

$$
r_{ij}(n) = \sum_{k=1}^{m} r_{ik}(n-1)\, p_{kj}
$$

  - With random initial state:

$$
\mathbf{P}(X_n = j) = \sum_{i=1}^{m} \mathbf{P}(X_0 = i)\, r_{ij}(n)
$$

[DERIVATION GAP: the key recursion is stated, not derived — the total-probability /
Markov-property argument
$\mathbf{P}(X_n=j\mid X_0=i)=\sum_k \mathbf{P}(X_{n-1}=k\mid X_0=i)\mathbf{P}(X_n=j\mid X_{n-1}=k)$
is only illustrated by the trellis picture. The initial condition of the recursion
($r_{ij}(0)=\delta_{ij}$, or equivalently $r_{ij}(1)=p_{ij}$) is not written on the slide.]

## Slide 7 — Recurrent and transient states (raster p02)

- State $i$ is **recurrent** if:
  starting from $i$,
  and from wherever you can go,
  there is a way of returning to $i$
- If not recurrent, called **transient**

[FIGURE: An eight-state transition diagram with no numerical probabilities, arranged in three
groups.
(a) **Far left:** a single node (state 5) carrying a self-loop (tall oval above it, arrowhead
returning into the node). It has one incoming arrow (see below) and no outgoing arrow other
than the self-loop — it is absorbing.
(b) **Middle:** four nodes at the corners of a square — top-left (state 3), top-right
(state 4), bottom-left (state 1), bottom-right (state 2). Edges: a straight horizontal arrow
$3 \to 4$ across the top (arrowhead on the right, one direction only); a pair of vertical arcs

$1 \to 3$); a pair of vertical arcs on the right side joining 4 and 2 in **both** directions
(down-arrow $4 \to 2$, up-arrow $2 \to 4$); and a pair of horizontal arcs along the bottom
joining 1 and 2 in **both** directions (upper arc $1 \to 2$ arrowhead right, lower arc
$2 \to 1$ arrowhead left). A long diagonal arrow leaves the bottom-left node 1 and points
**up-left into the far-left node 5**. A long diagonal arrow leaves the bottom-right node 2 and
points **up-right into node 6** of the right-hand group.
(c) **Far right:** three nodes. Node 6 (left of the group, mid height) has a self-loop (tall
oval above it). Node 7 (right of the group, mid height) also has a self-loop (tall oval above

  - $i$ transient:
    $\mathbf{P}(X_n = i) \to 0$,
    $i$ visited finite number of times

- **Recurrent class**:
  collection of recurrent states that
  "communicate" with each other
  and with no other state

2. **Eight-state diagram, exact digit placement.** The PDF text layer emits the labels in the
   groups "3 4", "6 7 5", "1 2", "8". The geometric row structure forces $\{3,4\}$ = top of
   the square, $\{1,2\}$ = bottom of the square, and $8$ = the lowest right-hand node; within
   the middle row the assignment $5$ = far-left absorbing node, $6,7$ = the right-hand pair is
   the reading adopted here (it is the only one giving the conventional numbering "transient
   states $1$–$4$, then the recurrent classes"). The *topology* of the figure — which is what
   matters — is read directly off the raster and is not in doubt.
3. **"$0.5$" vs "$0.8$" / "$0.2$" on the two-state example** were read at 4× magnification and
   are unambiguous, as are the "$0.4$" and the two "$0.3$" labels on the four-state
   convergence diagram.
4. Arc labels "1" on the three-state periodic diagram render as a thin serif "l"-like glyph at
   page resolution; magnification confirms they are the digit **1** (and mathematically they
   must be, since states 1 and 3 have no other outgoing transitions).
