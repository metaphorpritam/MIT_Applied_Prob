## Slide 2 — Review (raster p01)

- Assume a single class of recurrent states, aperiodic;
  plus transient states. Then,

$$\lim_{n \to \infty} r_{ij}(n) = \pi_j$$

  where $\pi_j$ does not depend on the initial conditions:

$$\lim_{n \to \infty} \mathbf{P}(X_n = j \mid X_0 = i) = \pi_j$$

- $\pi_1, \ldots, \pi_m$ can be found as the unique solution to the balance equations

$$\pi_j = \sum_k \pi_k p_{kj}, \qquad j = 1, \ldots, m,$$

  together with

$$\sum_j \pi_j = 1$$

## Slide 7 — Mean first passage and recurrence times (raster p02)

- Chain with one recurrent class; fix $s$ recurrent

- **Mean first passage time from $i$ to $s$:**

$$t_i \;=\; \mathbf{E}\big[\min\{n \ge 0 \text{ such that } X_n = s\} \mid X_0 = i\big]$$

- $t_1, t_2, \ldots, t_m$ are the unique solution to

$$\begin{aligned}
t_s &= 0, \\
t_i &= 1 + \sum_j p_{ij}\, t_j, \qquad \text{for all } i \ne s
\end{aligned}$$

- **Mean recurrence time of $s$:**

$$t_s^* \;=\; \mathbf{E}\big[\min\{n \ge 1 \text{ such that } X_n = s\} \mid X_0 = s\big]$$

- $\displaystyle t_s^* \;=\; 1 + \sum_j p_{sj}\, t_j$

[DERIVATION GAP: The slide does not derive the mean-recurrence-time formula $t_s^* = 1 + \sum_j p_{sj} t_j$ (one-step conditioning on the first transition out of $s$), nor does it state or prove the relation $\pi_s = 1/t_s^*$.]

### Footnotes on ambiguous symbols

1. Slide 4: the Greek letters were resolved from context — $\lambda$ (Poisson arrival rate), $\mu$ (exponential service/call-completion parameter), $\delta$ (small time-slot length), $B$ (number of lines). The diagram labels read $\lambda\delta$ (forward/arrival transition) and $i\mu\delta$ (backward/departure transition from state $i$); the leading character of the second label is the index $i$, not the digit 1, consistent with the balance equation $\lambda\pi_{i-1} = i\mu\pi_i$ printed on the same slide.
2. Slide 6: the symbol for expected time to absorption is $\mu_i$ (Greek mu with subscript $i$), not $u_i$ — matched to the "$\mu_i = 0$" and "$\mu_i = 1 + \sum_j p_{ij}\mu_j$" lines. Note this reuses $\mu$, which on slide 4 denoted the exponential parameter; the two usages are unrelated.
3. Slides 5–6: node circles in the transition diagrams are printed empty in the handout — no state numbers appear in the raster. The numbering used in the accompanying text ("state 4", "$i = 4$", "$i = 5$") refers to the two absorbing states on slide 5; the mapping of numbers to drawn circles is not shown on the slide itself.
4. Slide 7: the starred symbol is $t_s^*$ (mean recurrence time), distinct from the unstarred $t_s$ (which equals 0 by definition).
