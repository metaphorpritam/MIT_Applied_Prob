- MAP estimate: $\hat\theta_{\mathrm{MAP}}$ maximizes $f_{\Theta\mid X}(\theta\mid x)$
- LMS estimation:
  - $\hat\Theta = \mathbf{E}[\Theta\mid X]$ minimizes $\mathbf{E}\big[(\Theta - g(X))^2\big]$ over all estimators $g(\cdot)$
  - for any $x$, $\hat\theta = \mathbf{E}[\Theta\mid X = x]$ minimizes $\mathbf{E}\big[(\Theta - \hat\theta)^2 \mid X = x\big]$ over all estimates $\hat\theta$

[DERIVATION GAP: the slide asserts, without proof, that the conditional expectation minimizes the mean squared error (both the conditional version, for fixed $x$, and the unconditional version over all estimator functions $g(\cdot)$). The variational argument — expand $\mathbf{E}[(\Theta-\hat\theta)^2\mid X=x]$, differentiate in $\hat\theta$, set to zero to get $\hat\theta=\mathbf{E}[\Theta\mid X=x]$; then apply the tower rule to lift the pointwise result to all $g(\cdot)$ — is left to the reader/lecture.]

## Slide 4 — Some properties of LMS estimation (raster p01)

- Estimator: $\hat\Theta = \mathbf{E}[\Theta\mid X]$
- Estimation error: $\tilde\Theta = \hat\Theta - \Theta$

- $\mathbf{E}[\tilde\Theta] = 0$  and  $\mathbf{E}[\tilde\Theta\mid X = x] = 0$
- $\mathbf{E}[\tilde\Theta\, h(X)] = 0$, for any function $h$
- $\mathrm{cov}(\tilde\Theta, \hat\Theta) = 0$
- Since $\Theta = \hat\Theta - \tilde\Theta$:
  $$\mathrm{var}(\Theta) = \mathrm{var}(\hat\Theta) + \mathrm{var}(\tilde\Theta)$$

[DERIVATION GAP: each property is stated with no proof. Skipped: (i) $\mathbf{E}[\tilde\Theta\mid X=x] = \mathbf{E}[\hat\Theta - \Theta\mid X=x] = \mathbf{E}[\Theta\mid X=x] - \mathbf{E}[\Theta\mid X=x] = 0$, then the tower rule gives $\mathbf{E}[\tilde\Theta]=0$; (ii) $\mathbf{E}[\tilde\Theta h(X)] = \mathbf{E}\big[h(X)\mathbf{E}[\tilde\Theta\mid X]\big] = 0$; (iii) $\mathrm{cov}(\tilde\Theta,\hat\Theta) = \mathbf{E}[\tilde\Theta\hat\Theta]$ is the special case $h(X)=\mathbf{E}[\Theta\mid X]$; (iv) the variance decomposition then follows by taking variances of $\Theta = \hat\Theta - \tilde\Theta$ with the zero covariance cross-term.]

## Slide 5 — Linear LMS (raster p02)

- Consider estimators of $\Theta$, of the form $\hat\Theta = aX + b$
- Minimize $\mathbf{E}\big[(\Theta - aX - b)^2\big]$
- Best choice of $a,b$; best linear estimator:
  $$\hat\Theta_L = \mathbf{E}[\Theta] + \frac{\mathrm{Cov}(X,\Theta)}{\mathrm{var}(X)}\big(X - \mathbf{E}[X]\big)$$

[FIGURE: The same joint-support parallelogram figure as on Slides 2-3, redrawn once more: horizontal axis $x$, vertical axis $\theta$ (axes as arrows crossing at the origin, the strip floating up and to the right). Slanted strip of slope 1 and horizontal width 2, bottom edge at $\theta = 4$ spanning $x=3$ to $5$, top edge at $\theta = 10$ spanning $x=9$ to $11$. Here it illustrates fitting the best *straight line* through the joint distribution. | raster/L22_p02.png]

[DERIVATION GAP: the optimal $a,b$ are quoted, not derived. Skipped: minimize over $b$ first for fixed $a$ (giving $b = \mathbf{E}[\Theta] - a\mathbf{E}[X]$), substitute, then minimize the resulting quadratic in $a$ to get $a = \mathrm{Cov}(X,\Theta)/\mathrm{var}(X)$.]

## Slide 6 — Linear LMS properties (raster p02)

$$\hat\Theta_L = \mathbf{E}[\Theta] + \frac{\mathrm{Cov}(X,\Theta)}{\mathrm{var}(X)}\big(X - \mathbf{E}[X]\big)$$

$$\mathbf{E}[(\hat\Theta_L - \Theta)^2] = (1 - \rho^2)\,\sigma_\Theta^2$$

(Nothing else is printed on this slide: the two displayed equations above are its entire content. Verified against the raster: the subscript on $\sigma^2$ is $\Theta$ — i.e. $\sigma_\Theta^2 = \mathrm{var}(\Theta)$, the *prior* variance of $\Theta$, **not** $\sigma_{\tilde\Theta}^2$ — and the exponent on $\rho$ is $2$.)

[DERIVATION GAP: the mean squared error formula $(1-\rho^2)\sigma_\Theta^2$ is stated with no derivation, and $\rho$ is never defined on the slide — the slide never writes it down; it is $\rho = \mathrm{Cov}(X,\Theta)/(\sigma_X\sigma_\Theta)$, the correlation coefficient of $X$ and $\Theta$. Nor is it remarked on the slide that $|\rho| = 1$ gives zero error and $\rho = 0$ gives error $\sigma_\Theta^2$ (the estimator collapses to $\hat\Theta_L = \mathbf{E}[\Theta]$).]

## Slide 7 — Linear LMS with multiple data (raster p02)

- Consider estimators of the form:
  $$\hat\Theta = a_1X_1 + \cdots + a_nX_n + b$$
- Find best choices of $a_1,\ldots,a_n, b$
- Minimize:
  $$\mathbf{E}[(a_1X_1 + \cdots + a_nX_n + b - \Theta)^2]$$
- Set derivatives to zero — linear system in $b$ and the $a_i$
- Only means, variances, covariances matter

[DERIVATION GAP: the resulting normal equations / linear system are never written out; the slide only says "set derivatives to zero, linear system in $b$ and the $a_i$".]

## Slide 8 — The cleanest linear LMS example (raster p02)

$X_i = \Theta + W_i$, with $\Theta, W_1,\ldots,W_n$ independent

$\Theta \sim \mu,\ \sigma_0^2 \qquad W_i \sim 0,\ \sigma_i^2$

(i.e. $\Theta$ has mean $\mu$ and variance $\sigma_0^2$; $W_i$ has mean $0$ and variance $\sigma_i^2$ — no distributional form assumed, only first and second moments)

$$\hat\Theta_L = \frac{\displaystyle \mu/\sigma_0^2 + \sum_{i=1}^{n} X_i/\sigma_i^2}{\displaystyle \sum_{i=0}^{n} 1/\sigma_i^2}$$

(weighted average of $\mu, X_1,\ldots,X_n$)

- If all normal, $\hat\Theta_L = \mathbf{E}[\Theta\mid X_1,\ldots,X_n]$

[DERIVATION GAP: the closed-form weighted-average answer is quoted without solving the linear system of Slide 7 for this special structure. Note the index ranges: the numerator sum runs $i = 1$ to $n$, the denominator sum runs $i = 0$ to $n$ (so the prior precision $1/\sigma_0^2$ appears in the denominator).]

## Slide 10 — Big picture (raster p02)

- **Standard examples:**
  - $X_i$ uniform on $[0,\theta]$; uniform prior on $\theta$
  - $X_i$ Bernoulli($p$); uniform (or Beta) prior on $p$
  - $X_i$ normal with mean $\theta$, known variance $\sigma^2$; normal prior on $\theta$; $X_i = \Theta + W_i$

- **Estimation methods:**
  - MAP
  - MSE
  - Linear MSE
