## Slide 2 — The Bernoulli process (raster p01)

**The Bernoulli process**

- A sequence of independent Bernoulli trials
- At each trial, $i$:
  - $\mathbf{P}(\text{success}) = \mathbf{P}(X_i = 1) = p$
  - $\mathbf{P}(\text{failure}) = \mathbf{P}(X_i = 0) = 1 - p$

- Examples:
  - Sequence of lottery wins/losses
  - Sequence of ups and downs of the Dow Jones
  - Arrivals (each second) to a bank
  - Arrivals (at each time slot) to server

## Footnotes on ambiguous symbols

1. The success-probability symbol is $p$ (lowercase italic p) throughout; probability operators are set in bold roman, $\mathbf{P}(\cdot)$, and expectation as $\mathbf{E}[\cdot]$ — the slides distinguish these typographically.
2. On slide 5/6, the time index is $t$ (Roman t) and the success-count index is $k$; $Y_k$ (subscript k) is the time of the $k$th success, not $Y_h$ or $Y_R$.
3. On slide 3 the process index alternates between $i$ (slide 2, "At each trial, $i$") and $t$ (slide 3, $X_t$); both refer to the trial/time index. This is as printed.
4. Arrival markers in the figures on slides 7 and 8 are small open circles sitting *between* two adjacent ticks (i.e. inside a slot), not on top of a tick. The slides carry no numeric axis labels and no axis captions; the 1-indexed slot numbers given in the [FIGURE] blocks were counted off the raster and are supplied only so the diagrams can be redrawn faithfully.
5. Direction of the connecting arrows is the load-bearing detail in both figures and is easy to invert: in **splitting** the arrows point *away from* the middle (original) axis, up and down; in **merging** the arrows point *toward* the middle (merged) axis, from the top and bottom inputs. In both figures the middle axis carries the same arrival pattern (slots 2, 4, 7, 8, 12).
